The cause

Anki renders math with MathJax, using \( … \) for inline formulas and \[ … \] for formulas on their own line. Dollar signs are not enabled by default, so a card that contains $E = mc^2$ shows the dollars and the raw code.

ChatGPT, Claude and most other assistants write math with dollar signs because that is the usual Markdown convention. The mismatch is the whole problem.

The fix, before import

Paste the AI's answer into the tool on this page with the math option ticked. Inline $…$ becomes \( … \) and $$…$$ becomes \[ … \]. Amounts such as $5 or $10 are left alone, because a price is not a formula.

For cards already in your collection, Anki's Find and Replace can work with regular expressions. It is powerful but a wrong pattern can damage many notes at once, so back up your collection before trying it.

  • Inline: $x^2$ → \(x^2\)
  • Display: $$\int_0^1 x\,dx$$ → \[\int_0^1 x\,dx\]
  • Already correct \( … \) formulas are kept as they are.

Questions

Is AI to Anki free?

Yes. No account, no card limit, no watermark.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The deck is built inside your browser, on your device. Nothing you paste leaves your computer or phone.

Why do my formulas show dollar signs in Anki?

Anki's MathJax only reads \( … \) and \[ … \], while AI tools usually write $…$. This tool converts them for you and leaves prices like $5 alone.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the .apkg file with AnkiDroid on Android or AnkiMobile on iPhone and iPad. On a computer, double-click it to open it in Anki.

Can I make cloze (fill-in-the-blank) cards?

Yes. Keep the {{c1::…}} syntax: each line with a cloze becomes a cloze note, with one card per number.

Which AI works best?

Any of them: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, Copilot or DeepSeek. The format matters more than the model, so start with the prompt above.
