How to get flashcards from Claude

Start a new chat, paste the prompt from this page, then paste your notes, a lecture transcript or a chapter. Ask for one idea per card: short cards are easier to review and easier to fix.

When Claude has answered, use the copy button under its message and paste the whole answer into the box above. You don't need to remove the introduction or the closing sentence: the tool ignores text that isn't a card.

  • Long document: split it into chapters and make one deck per chapter.
  • Want fill-in-the-blank cards? Ask Claude to write them with {{c1::…}}.
  • Formulas: ask for $…$; the tool converts them to Anki's \( … \) format.

Why not copy the cards into Anki by hand?

Copying 50 cards one by one takes longer than generating them. Importing a text file into Anki works, but you have to choose a separator, map the fields and fix the formulas yourself. Here the deck, the note types and the formulas are handled for you, and the file opens on every Anki app.

Questions

Is AI to Anki free?

Yes. No account, no card limit, no watermark.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The deck is built inside your browser, on your device. Nothing you paste leaves your computer or phone.

Why do my formulas show dollar signs in Anki?

Anki's MathJax only reads \( … \) and \[ … \], while AI tools usually write $…$. This tool converts them for you and leaves prices like $5 alone.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the .apkg file with AnkiDroid on Android or AnkiMobile on iPhone and iPad. On a computer, double-click it to open it in Anki.

Can I make cloze (fill-in-the-blank) cards?

Yes. Keep the {{c1::…}} syntax: each line with a cloze becomes a cloze note, with one card per number.

Which AI works best?

Any of them: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, Copilot or DeepSeek. The format matters more than the model, so start with the prompt above.
